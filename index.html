Why Slingerland?

We bridge the gap between clinical intervention and general education. Our method is:

  • Evidence-Aligned: Rooted deeply in the Science of Reading.
  • Multisensory: Simultaneously engages visual, auditory, and kinesthetic-motor pathways.
  • Classroom-Implementable: Adaptable to any existing curriculum or text.
  • Preventative & Remedial: Supports diverse learners, including those with dyslexia, before they fail.

The Slingerland Approach

A jargon-free, simultaneous, multisensory method. We teach the structure of the English language explicitly, encompassing all five pillars of the Science of Reading.

Phonemic Awareness

Hearing and manipulating individual sounds.

Phonics

Mapping sounds to letters for encoding & decoding.

Fluency

Reading with accuracy, speed, and expression.

Vocabulary

Understanding words in isolation and context.

Comprehension

Extracting meaning and facts from text.

How it looks in practice: The Learning Channels

Auditory (Hearing)

Vocalizing words, identifying syllables, and explicitly learning English structure.

Visual (Seeing)

Decoding by marking vowels, dividing syllables, and preparing for fluent reading.

Kinesthetic (Doing)

Air tracing, cursive connections, and encoding seamlessly from oral to paper.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does it differ from other approaches? ▾
Unlike programs that are strictly 1-on-1, Slingerland adapts Orton-Gillingham for the general classroom. It acts preventatively for all students while providing the rigorous structure dyslexic students need.
Where can it be used? ▾
It is highly flexible. It is designed for whole classrooms, but is equally effective in small resource groups, special education pull-outs, and 1-on-1 tutoring.
Is it compatible with school initiatives? ▾
Yes. Because Slingerland is an approach and not just a boxed curriculum, the instructional techniques can be applied to any existing school reading materials or content area.
